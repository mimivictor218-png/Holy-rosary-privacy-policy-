Overview

Holy Rosary is a Catholic prayer application designed to guide you through the full Rosary, Litany, Novenas, and Chaplet of Divine Mercy. Your spiritual journey is private — we collect zero personal data. This policy explains how the app respects your privacy while offering voice assistance and local preferences.

🕊️ Core promise: Holy Rosary does not track, profile, or sell any information. Internet access is used only for Android's built-in Text-to-Speech engine when you enable Voice Mode — no data is sent to our servers or any third party.

Data Collection — Absolutely No Personal Data

Holy Rosary does not collect any personal data whatsoever. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, device ID, or any identifiable information. No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are present in the app.

What the app explicitly does NOT do:

  • ❌ Collect names, emails, or contact details
  • ❌ Track location or IP addresses
  • ❌ Access contacts, camera, microphone, or storage files
  • ❌ Share any data with third parties — because there is no data to share
  • ❌ Use cookies, fingerprinting, or persistent identifiers
  • ❌ Send any usage statistics or telemetry

The only internet permission declared is for Android's Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine — a system service that requires internet to download or enhance voices. Holy Rosary never communicates with any external server.

Internet & Permissions — Why and How

To provide optional voice assistance and the 30-second silent prayer bell, Holy Rosary requests two permissions, both explained clearly:

  • 🔗 INTERNET — Required solely to allow Android's built-in Text-to-Speech engine (e.g., Google TTS) to download language voices or improve speech synthesis when you enable Voice Mode. The app itself does not send or receive any data over the internet. No servers are contacted by Holy Rosary.
  • 📳 VIBRATE — Used exclusively to produce a gentle vibration (bell simulation) after the 30-second silent prayer, helping you know when to continue. No other haptic feedback is used.
🔒 Important: If you never enable Voice Mode, the internet permission is never utilized. The app works completely offline except for that optional feature. We do not collect information about your TTS usage.

Local Storage — Your Preferences Stay on Your Device

Holy Rosary saves minimal settings locally on your Android device using standard SharedPreferences. This includes:

  • 🌐 Your chosen language (English, Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa, Latin, Español, Português)
  • 🌙 Dark / Light theme preference
  • 🔊 Voice Mode on/off and voice speed setting
  • 🕯 Optional: last prayed mystery reference (just to resume, not identifiable)

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any cloud, not accessible by the developer, and can be cleared at any time via the app's Settings screen or Android app settings.

No prayer text, personal notes, or sensitive information is ever stored.

Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Holy Rosary does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social media. The app contains no Facebook, Google Analytics, Firebase, AdMob, or similar trackers.

  • 📦 No external libraries that collect data are used.
  • 🚫 No content delivery networks (CDNs) are accessed.
  • 🤝 No data sharing with advertisers, churches, or organizations.

The only system interaction is with Android's TTS engine (a built-in component), which is governed by your device's own privacy controls.

Children's Privacy — Family-Friendly

Holy Rosary is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we collect no personal information, there is no need for parental consent under COPPA (US) or GDPR-K (EU). The app does not contain ads, in-app purchases, or any features that could compromise a child's privacy.

Parents can confidently allow children to pray the Rosary using this app without supervision regarding data collection.

Data Security

Since Holy Rosary does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of data breach from our side. The local preferences stored on your device are protected by Android's standard app sandbox. No external server or cloud backend exists, making the app inherently secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Because we never collect data, updates will not materially affect your privacy. For significant changes, we may notify users via an in-app notice (no data transmission) or by updating the Google Play Store listing.
